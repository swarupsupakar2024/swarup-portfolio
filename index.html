About

I'm a Computer Science Engineer with an M.Tech in Information Technology from MAKAUT, West Bengal. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning and production software: given a dataset or a problem statement, I care about shipping something that actually runs, not just a notebook that proves a point.

My thesis built a multi-model crop recommendation engine from a 12,200-record agricultural dataset, benchmarked across nine ML algorithms and deployed as a live Django web app. Alongside that, I've published first-author research on handwritten digit recognition, and shipped smaller full-stack tools with Flask, Node.js and vanilla JavaScript.

I'm currently looking for a Machine Learning Engineer or Python Developer role where I can take real data problems from exploration through to deployment — and I'm equally comfortable owning the backend and the interface around a model.
